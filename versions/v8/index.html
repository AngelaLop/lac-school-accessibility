The Problem

🌏

Geography as Barrier

Mountains, rivers, and dense rainforest isolate millions of children from their nearest school, turning short distances into hours-long journeys.

📋

Invisible Gaps

Traditional enrollment data cannot reveal whether a child must walk two hours each way. Without travel-time metrics, inequity hides in plain sight.

⚠️

Dropout Risk

When the journey to school is dangerous or exhausting, attendance plummets and dropout rates soar — especially for girls and indigenous communities.

How It Works

Four layers of geospatial analysis, combined into a single accessibility score.

🗺
1

School Mapping

Geolocate every registered school using national ministry data and satellite verification.

🌎
2

Friction Surfaces

Model terrain difficulty — elevation, land cover, road networks, rivers — at high resolution.

⏱
3

Travel-Time Engine

Calculate realistic walking and transit times from every populated pixel to the nearest school.

📈
4

Accessibility Index

Score and rank communities by access level, highlighting critical gaps for policy action.

Impact: Panama Pilot

In the Comarca Ngäbe-Buglé, our analysis revealed that over 60% of children live more than 90 minutes from their nearest secondary school. This finding directly informed Panama’s Ministry of Education infrastructure investment plan.

17 New school sites identified
40% Reduction in avg. travel time
85K Students impacted
