The Challenge

When the road to school is the hardest lesson

Millions of children across Latin America face impossible journeys to school every day. They navigate rivers, mountains, and unpaved roads just to sit in a classroom.

Rural girls are disproportionately affected, often pulled from school first when the journey becomes too dangerous or too long. Until now, the granular data needed to understand these barriers simply did not exist.

The LAC School Accessibility Platform harmonizes data across 21 countries, transforming scattered records into a unified picture of educational access across an entire region.

How It Works

The Friction Map Methodology

1

Locate every school

We harmonized official school registries from 21 countries, cleaning and georeferencing 532,650 schools with precise coordinates.

2

Map the terrain

Using satellite data, we built friction surfaces that capture the real cost of travel: paved roads, dirt paths, rivers, mountains, and forests.

3

Calculate travel time

The FMM algorithm finds the fastest realistic path from every point on the map to the nearest school, producing travel-time isochrones that reveal exactly which communities are underserved.
