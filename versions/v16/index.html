The Challenge

When the road to school is the hardest lesson

Impossible journeys

Millions of children navigate rivers, mountains, and unpaved roads just to sit in a classroom. For families without transportation, a school 15 km away might as well be on another continent.

Rural girls disproportionately affected

When the journey becomes too dangerous or too long, girls are often the first pulled from school. The distance creates a silent crisis of inequality.

Data that didn’t exist

Until now, policymakers lacked the granular data needed to understand where barriers exist. The platform harmonizes data across 21 countries into one unified picture.

How It Works

The Friction Map Methodology

Three steps from raw data to real understanding.

1

Locate every school

We harmonized official school registries from 21 countries, cleaning and georeferencing 532,650 schools with precise coordinates.

2

Map the terrain

Using satellite data, we built friction surfaces that capture the real cost of travel: paved roads, dirt paths, rivers, mountains, and forests.

3

Calculate travel time

The FMM algorithm finds the fastest realistic path from every point on the map to the nearest school, producing travel-time isochrones that reveal exactly which communities are underserved.
