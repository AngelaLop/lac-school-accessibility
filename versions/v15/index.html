The Solution

Data that didn’t exist before

The LAC School Accessibility Platform harmonizes official school registries from 21 countries, creating a unified dataset of 532,650 schools with precise coordinates.

For the first time, policymakers can see exactly where accessibility gaps exist and target investment where it matters most.

The Challenge

Millions face impossible journeys

In the most remote corners of Latin America, children navigate rivers, mountains, and unpaved roads just to sit in a classroom.

Rural girls are disproportionately affected, often pulled from school first when the journey becomes too dangerous or too long.

How It Works

Friction Map Methodology

1

Locate every school

Harmonized registries from 21 countries, 532,650 schools georeferenced.

2

Map the terrain

Satellite friction surfaces capturing roads, rivers, mountains, forests.

3

Calculate travel time

FMM algorithm produces travel-time isochrones for every community.

Why It Matters

What this means for Yurani

Traditional metrics like enrollment rates mask the daily reality. A school that is 15 km away might as well be on another continent without roads.

The FMM captures actual walking conditions, not straight-line distance. It reveals the lived reality of terrain, waterways, and absent infrastructure.

For the first time, we can see the invisible walls that separate children like Yurani from their education.
