♦ Methodology FMM Model

The Friction Map Model (FMM) calculates travel time from every point in a territory to the nearest school. It combines land-cover data, road networks, topography, and waterways into a single friction surface, then computes least-cost-path travel times across multiple transport modes (walking, motorized, mixed).

Data Sources
OpenStreetMap, national registries, satellite land-cover imagery
Resolution
1 km grid cells across full national territories
Transport Modes
Walking, motorized, and combined multimodal scenarios
Validation
Cross-checked with census microdata and household surveys
